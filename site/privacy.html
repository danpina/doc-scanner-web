Privacy Policy

Doc Scanner · Last updated October 26, 2026

Doc Scanner lets you scan documents with your phone, crop and filter them, and export them as a PDF. In short: your documents stay on your device. We don't collect them, and we don't collect anything else about you.

The iPhone app (version 1.1 and later)

  • No account, no sign-in. The app doesn't ask for an email address, name or password.
  • Everything is processed on your iPhone. Photos, camera images and PDFs you add are scanned, cropped, filtered and turned into PDFs on the device. They are never uploaded.
  • Text recognition happens on the device too. When you export a scan, the app reads the words on the pages with Apple's on-device Vision framework, so the PDF can be searched and its text selected. The recognized text is kept with your saved scans on the iPhone, only to power search in the app. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Saved scans live in the app's storage on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud Backup, Apple includes them in your device backup like other app data; that is between you and Apple. Deleting the app deletes the scans saved in it.
  • The app itself never contacts a server, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We don't know who uses it or what they scan. The Privacy Policy and Support links in Settings open this website in your browser.
  • Apple's document camera (part of iOS) is used to find the page edges. Camera access is only used while you scan.

This website

The website scanner works the same way: everything happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Scans you choose to save are stored in your browser's own storage on your device (IndexedDB). We cannot see them. Your browser may delete that storage if you stop visiting the site, so download any PDF you want to keep. You can remove saved scans from the site at any time, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

The site is static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of your visit, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and has no tracking. The optional "Buy me a coffee" link opens PayPal, which handles anything you choose to do there.

Earlier versions of the iPhone app (1.0)

Version 1.0 offered optional accounts. If you created one, we stored your email address, a one-way hash of your password (or Apple's anonymous identifier, if you used Sign in with Apple) and the PDFs you chose to save, on servers run for us by Render and Turso. A password-reset email, if you asked for one, was sent through Brevo. That account service is being retired and the data deleted. Version 1.1 no longer uses it. If you had an account and want your data deleted straight away, or want a copy of your saved scans first, email us at the address below and we'll do it.

Children

Doc Scanner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. (We don't collect information from anyone.)
