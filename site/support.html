Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Doc Scanner has no accounts. Open the app and start scanning.

Where are my scans saved?
Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded. If iCloud Backup is on, they are part of your iPhone's backup.

How do I keep a copy of a scan?
Open My Scans, swipe a scan to the right (or press and hold it) and choose Share, then Save to Files, or send it by email, Messages or AirDrop. Deleting the app deletes the scans saved in it, so share the ones you want to keep first.

How do I delete a scan?
In My Scans, swipe it to the left, or press and hold it, and choose Delete. There is nothing else to delete: no account, no server copy.

How do I change the order of the pages?
In the scan, touch and hold a page, then drag it onto another page. The arrow buttons under each page also move it one step.

The Scan button says the camera isn't available.
Document scanning needs a physical iPhone, and camera access must be allowed for Doc Scanner in iOS Settings. You can always add photos or a PDF instead.

I used version 1.0 with an account. What happened to my scans?
Version 1.0 could save scans to an account on our server; version 1.1 no longer does. Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll send you your saved scans, or delete them if you prefer. See the Privacy Policy.

Where is my data stored?
See the Privacy Policy. In short: on your device only.
