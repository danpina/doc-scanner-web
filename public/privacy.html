Privacy Policy

Doc Scanner · Last updated October 5, 2026

Doc Scanner lets you scan documents with your phone, crop and filter them, and export them as a PDF. This page explains what the app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and a password, stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form. If you sign in with Apple, we receive Apple's anonymous user identifier and the email address you choose to share, which may be a private relay address.
  • Saved scans (only if you tap Save to My Scans): the PDF you created, its title, page count, size and the date you saved it.

Photos, camera images and PDFs you import are processed on your device. They are not uploaded unless you choose to save the resulting PDF to your account. Using the app as a guest stores nothing on our servers. We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, we do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in and to keep your saved scans available on your devices. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Our hosting providers store and serve the data: Render runs the service, and Turso (on Amazon Web Services in Ireland) stores the database.
  • Brevo, our email provider, receives your email address only when you ask for a password-reset link, so it can deliver that one email for us.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, and provides the document camera used to scan.

Cookies

The website uses a single cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account details and all of your saved scans are permanently deleted and, if you signed in with Apple, we revoke the connection to your Apple ID.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account.
  • Delete individual scans from My Scans.
  • You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete your data by emailing us at the address below.

Children

Doc Scanner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
