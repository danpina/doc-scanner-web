Common questions

The app is slow to open or sign in.
The server can go to sleep when it hasn't been used for a while, and the first request may take up to a minute to wake it. After that it's fast.

Do I need an account?
No. Choose Continue as Guest to scan, crop, filter and export PDFs without signing in. An account is only needed to save scans so they're there next time.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your saved scans. You can also email us.

I forgot my password.
Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll send you a temporary password. Log in with it, then change it in the app under Settings → Change password, or on the website under Account. If you signed up with Apple there is no password — just use Sign in with Apple again (or choose Settings → Set a password to also log in with your email).
